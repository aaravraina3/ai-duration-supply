# Findings

The only place a number is stated as established. Everything else links here.

Rewritten 23 September 2026 after the adversarial review (`09_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.md`).
Every number below comes from one sample, **2024-01-03 to 2026-09-22, n = 680
trading days**, set by `SAMPLE_END` in `src/config.py`. GSW-based results end at
2026-09-18 because GSW publishes with a lag. Numbers are generated by
`src/headline.py` into `output/tables/headline.json`, or by the named script.

**Status of all inference in this file: exploratory.** Several hundred test
statistics were computed on this sample and about fifteen had a prediction
written first (`10_PREREGISTRATION.md`). No p-value here survives a multiplicity
correction. The only confirmatory test this project will run is the frozen
held-out test in `src/holdout.py`.

Confidence labels, within that exploratory status:
- **A** survives leave-one-out, randomization and specification variation
- **B** point estimate robust, inference marginal
- **C** suggestive only
- **X** established negative result

## B. The duration channel

**B1. AI issuance raises the 10Y term premium on the announcement day.**

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Local projection, h = 0 | **+0.0812 bp per $bn** of 10-year equivalents |
| Newey-West t (6 lags) | 3.47 |
| Plain OLS t | 1.97 (p = 0.049) |
| Kish effective n | 9.7 |
| Randomization, uniform placebo | p = 0.078 |
| Randomization, placebo matched on lag after macro release | **p = 0.0825** |
| Randomization, matched on lag and release type | p = 0.1095 |
| Wild cluster bootstrap, 17 clusters, null imposed | p = 0.012 |
| Leave-one-out over 16 deals | +0.068 to +0.094, min t 2.63 |
| Drop the three overlapping pairs | +0.091 (t 3.39) |
| 2025 onward only | +0.063 (t 3.01) |
| Same test on CMT 10Y yield instead of ACM | +0.097 (t 3.48) |
| Floater duration 0 to 2 years | +0.0813 to +0.0807 |

The timing-matched placebo was the main open threat (review M1). Real deals
cluster 1-3 business days after macro releases and the term premium does drift
up after releases in this sample (+0.72 bp on release days, +0.72 bp at lag 2).
Matching placebo dates on that lag leaves the placebo centred at +0.0001, so
post-release drift does not produce the day-0 coefficient. It does move the
p-value from 0.078 to 0.08-0.11. **B**

**B2. The effect is transitory.** h = 2: +0.115 (t 3.24). h = 5: **+0.0125
(t 0.19)**. Against the timing-matched baseline the h = 5 response is below zero
(placebo mean +0.041 matched on lag, +0.099 on lag and type), so the effect does
not merely decay, it is gone relative to comparably timed days. Part of the h = 2
peak is post-FOMC drift: the lag-and-type matched placebo reproduces about half
of it (+0.058 of +0.115). Scaled by 2026 supply of 194.3 $bn, the impact effect
is **15.8 bp** summed across eight separate one-day events, and the effect
surviving to h = 5 is **2.4 bp**. **A** on transitoriness.

**B3. Treasury buybacks move the term premium the other way.** Raw long-bucket
series: −0.197 bp per $bn at h = 0 (t −1.53), randomization p = 0.316. But the
operation date and maximum size are announced beforehand, and 75% of long-bucket
operations since 2024 filled exactly to that maximum, so only **11.5%** of the
raw series was news on the day (review M4). The surprise-only shock, fill minus
expected fill, keeps the sign: h = 0 −0.39 (t −0.79), **h = 2 −1.45 (t −2.46)**,
h = 5 −0.72 (t −0.59), on 18 non-zero surprise days. Lou, Yan and Zhang (2013)
show pre-announced Treasury flows still move prices because dealer capacity is
limited, so the raw series measures the whole flow and the surprise series
measures the news. Both point the same way. **C**

**B4. REFUTED: the forward-curve separation of flow from premium.** The 20y10y
forward built from GSW looked nearly orthogonal to the cash 10Y (R² 0.062) and
carried the AI effect at t 3.20. Built from observed CMT points the same forward
has R² **0.623** with the 10Y, and the two versions correlate at **0.384**. The
orthogonality was a Svensson extrapolation artifact. The AI coefficient survives
on CMT (+0.133, t 3.36); what does not survive is the claim that it identifies a
mechanism. The buyback effect at 20y10y was inflated 2.3x by the artifact
(−0.660 GSW, −0.287 CMT). See `09_ADVERSARIAL_REVIEW.md` F1 and fig12. **X**

**B5. On-the-run spread: the move is not concentrated in on-the-run bonds.** NEW.
Rate-lock hedges around new corporate issues sell the most liquid Treasuries. CMT
yields are built from on-the-run securities, GSW par yields from seasoned paper
only, so CMT minus GSW at 10 years is a proxy for on-the-run cheapness. If
hedgers were selling on-the-runs, that spread should widen on deal days. It
barely moves: **+0.004 bp per $bn (t 0.77), 4% of the +0.097 yield move**. The
move is common to on-the-run and seasoned bonds. This rules out one version of
the flow story, cash hedging in the on-the-run issue. It does not rule out
futures-based hedging, because the cheapest-to-deliver note is usually a seasoned
issue that GSW also sees. **C**

**B6. Real yields move almost as much as nominal.** NEW. Without the breakeven
control, the 10Y real yield (TIPS) responds at 0.87 times the nominal 10Y, and
breakevens themselves barely move (+0.015, t 0.82). A nominal-only flow would
widen breakevens. With the breakeven control in, real and nominal coefficients
are mechanically identical at h = 0, because the breakeven is nominal minus
real; that version of the test is uninformative and is not used. The breakeven
control is kept in the headline because it was pre-specified and the shock does
not move it; dropping it raises TP10 at h = 0 to +0.095 (t 4.93). **C**

**B7. Weak auction demand raises the term premium.** Bid-to-cover surprise −4.49
(t −1.79), randomization p = 0.0705. Local projection over **all tenors** is the
primary: h = 0 **+4.50 per unit of weakness (t 1.81)**, n = 220 auction days. The
7-10y subsample (+14.76, t 2.59) was selected after seeing the cross-section and
is descriptive only (review M8). The tail proxy was contaminated (R² 0.390 with
the same-day 10Y move) and is not used. This is a known result: auction price
pressure is documented in Lou, Yan and Zhang (2013). **C**

**B8. Three flows, consistent signs, weak joint evidence.** Issuance up, buybacks
down, weak auctions up. Under the null each sign is a coin flip, so three
matching signs has probability 0.125, and Fisher-combining the three
randomization p-values gives p ≈ 0.046 before any multiplicity correction.
Coherent, not strong. **C**

## C. What cannot be established here

**C1. Maturity-localized concession is not identifiable from published curves.**
An injected 5 bp bump is recovered at 77.7% by the three-factor step alone and at
−10.7% after the Svensson re-fit GSW applies. The measured concession flips from
−0.167 bp to +0.123 bp as the exclusion band moves. Excluding event days from
the PCA changes nothing (loadings correlate at 0.99996). Needs TRACE or CRSP. **X**

**C2. The seven-event study is underpowered.** Placebo sd 1.74 bp gives a minimum
detectable effect of **3.4 bp** at 95%. The expected effect is 1 to 3 bp. **A**

**C3. X = +0.86 bp is one observation.** Leave-one-out range −0.090 to +1.626.
Dropping Meta 2024-08-07, which sits inside the August 2024 yen-carry unwind,
flips the sign. **X**

**C4. MBS is untested.** No free agency MBS issuance series was reachable. It is
half of the sell-side claim being tested. **X**

**C5. Flow versus premium is not fully identified.** B5 and B6 cut against the
simplest hedging story. Futures hedging and generic dealer-inventory pressure
remain possible. The clean test needs daily swap rates (available from BlueGamma
with an account, which this project did not create) or intraday Treasury prices
around pricing time. **X**

## D. Scale and the 2026 move

**D1. Y.** 10Y CMT from 4.19% on 2026-01-02: **+81 bp to 5.00% on 2026-09-15**,
the first 5% close since July 2007, and **+77 bp to 4.96% on 2026-09-22**, the
sample end. Press reports put the 10Y at 5.104% intraday on 2026-09-23, which is
not yet in FRED and not in any result here. **A**

**D2. The decomposition depends on the endpoint.** To 2026-09-15: ΔTP −8.6 bp,
ΔExpectations +79.0 bp. To 2026-09-22: **ΔTP −21.9 bp, ΔExpectations +86.9 bp**,
the extra week spanning the Fed hike. Across 25 start and end pairs ΔTP ranges
−28.6 to +23.9 bp and is positive in 12. Over 2024-2026 as a whole the 10Y rose
105 bp of which +103 bp was term premium. The only robust statement is that
within calendar-2026 windows expectations dominate. **A** on the sensitivity.

**D3. AI supply is a small share of duration supply.** 194.3 $bn of 10-year
equivalents in 2026, 9.4% of Treasury coupon duration supply (2,073.5 $bn). **A**

**D4. The Fed hike took the 5% crossing back.** 25 bp to 3.75-4.00% on
2026-09-16, unanimous. 10Y 5.01% on the day, 4.94% the next. **A** on the data.

**D5. Explained share.** Against Y to 2026-09-22 (+77 bp): announcement impact
15.8 bp, 20.5%, but transitory; persistent 2.4 bp, 3.2%; realised term premium
change −21.9 bp, −28.4%. None of these supports a persistent 30 bp supply effect
from this slice of issuance. **B**
